# BDVTrading — 2-Week WhatsApp Marketing Plan
### Group: BDVTrading (500 members) | Seller: bdv-giant | Period: Weeks 1–2

---

## Overview

This plan covers daily engagement tactics, quiz game formats, exclusive deals, and blitz sales for the BDVTrading WhatsApp community. The goal is to convert passive members into active buyers/traders and drive repeat purchases through the bdv-giant seller account.

---

## Week 1 — Warm-Up & Hype Building

### Day 1 — Monday: Grand Reveal Drop
**Message to group:**
> "BDVTrading is levelling up. This week: daily games, flash deals, and prizes. Stay active — lurkers become legends here. First game drops at 8PM."

**Game: Guess the MTG Card (Classic)**
Post a cropped/blurred image of an MTG card. First correct answer wins a 10% discount code on their next bdv-giant order.
- Post at 8PM
- Keep poll open for 1 hour
- Announce winner publicly in group

**Deal (bdv-giant):**
- Welcome Week Bundle: Buy any 3 singles → get 1 free random rare. Valid 24h only.
- Pin the deal in the group with a countdown sticker.

---

### Day 2 — Tuesday: Lore Tuesday
**Game: MTG Flavour Text Quiz**
Post a flavour text from a card (no image). Members guess the card name.
> *"He who controls the dead controls the living."*
First correct answer wins a 5% coupon.

**Engagement push:**
- Ask the group: "What's the card you've been hunting for weeks?" → Use answers to restock bdv-giant listings that same week.

---

### Day 3 — Wednesday: Blitz Sale #1
**⚡ BLITZ SALE — 3 HOURS ONLY ⚡**
- 20% off all Modern staples on bdv-giant
- First 10 buyers get a free sleeve pack
- Post at 12PM sharp, end at 3PM
- Countdown message at 2:45PM: *"15 minutes left!"*

**Game: Price is Right (MTG Edition)**
Post a card image. Members guess the current TCGPlayer market price closest without going over.
Winner gets a store credit of $5 on bdv-giant.

---

### Day 4 — Thursday: Community Night
**Game: 5-Card Hand Challenge**
Post 5 random cards. Members have 10 minutes to build the most creative/broken combo.
Best answer (voted by group reaction/emoji) wins a mystery card pack from bdv-giant.

**Deal:**
- "Deck Builder's Thursday": Any 10 cards from a single set = 15% off. No code needed — just tag @bdv-giant in order notes.

---

### Day 5 — Friday: FOMO Friday
**Blitz Sale #2 — Limited Stock Alert**
> "We pulled 12 copies of [HIGH DEMAND CARD]. First come, first served. Link below."
- Post at 6PM Friday
- Estimated sell-out in 2h
- Follow-up "SOLD OUT" message builds scarcity credibility

**Game: Blind Identify**
Post the rules text of a card only (name and art stripped). First to guess wins 15% off next order.

---

### Day 6 — Saturday: Trade Saturday
**Community Trade Post**
Pin a message:
> "Drop your HAVES and WANTS below. bdv-giant will match 3 trades today and cover shipping on one of them."

This drives group activity organically and gives bdv-giant visibility as a facilitator, not just a seller.

**Deal: Saturday Bundle**
- Pick any Commander pre-con on bdv-giant → get 3 free upgrade singles (auto-selected for the deck theme).

---

### Day 7 — Sunday: Recap & Teaser
**Weekly Recap post:**
- Announce the week's quiz winners
- Show leaderboard (track who's won what)
- Tease Week 2: "Next week: new game formats, a surprise mystery box drop, and a sealed product blitz."

## Week 2 — Escalation & Conversion

### Day 8 — Monday: Leaderboard Reveal
**Introduce a points system retroactively:**
- Quiz win = 3 pts
- Purchase = 2 pts per $10 spent
- Trade facilitated = 1 pt

**Announce top 3 players from Week 1.** Top player gets a $15 store credit on bdv-giant.

**New Game: Syled Draft Pick**
Post a pack of 8 cards (image collage). Members have 15 minutes to pick their "first pick" and explain why. Best reasoning wins store credit.

---

### Day 9 — Tuesday: Mystery Box Drop
**Mystery Box Blitz — Limited to 20 boxes**
> "$25 Mystery Box: 10 rares, 2 foils, 1 guaranteed $10+ card. Ships today if ordered before 5PM."
- bdv-giant lists the boxes
- Hype with unboxing poll: "Would you open it or trade it sealed?"
- Post at 10AM. 20 boxes typically move in under 4h with a warm 500-person group.

**Game: Oldest Card in the Room**
Members post the oldest MTG card they own (just the set symbol/name). Most vintage card (by set release date) wins a coupon.

---

### Day 10 — Wednesday: Educational Drop + Soft Sell
**"Did You Know?" Series:**
Post a short value insight:
> "Black Lotus has sold for $540,000 at auction. But did you know [CARD X] from Commander Masters is criminally underpriced right now at $4? We have 8 in stock."

Pair each insight with a direct bdv-giant listing link. This educates and converts without feeling like a hard sell.

**Game: Art Crop Identify**
Post a tight crop of MTG card art only (no text visible). First correct guess wins.

---

### Day 11 — Thursday: Group-Only Exclusive
**"BDVTrading Members Only" Deal:**
> "This deal is NOT public. Not on our store front. WhatsApp group only."
- 25% off a curated collection of 20 Legacy/Vintage staples
- DM bdv-giant with code **BDVFAM** to claim
- 48h window

This exclusivity mechanic rewards group loyalty and encourages members to stay active so they don't miss future drops.

**Game: Build-a-Deck Budget Challenge**
> "$50 budget. What's your strongest possible deck? Post your list."
Community votes. Winner gets $10 store credit.

---

### Day 12 — Friday: Biggest Blitz of the Two Weeks
**⚡⚡ MEGA BLITZ — 2 HOURS ONLY ⚡⚡**
Rules:
- 30% off everything site-wide on bdv-giant
- Starts 7PM sharp
- First 5 orders get a bonus foil rare
- Message goes out at 6:30PM as a teaser: *"Something big in 30 minutes."*
- Message at 6:55PM: *"5 minutes."*
- Message at 7PM: *"GO."*

---

### Day 13 — Saturday: Social Proof Saturday
**Collect and post testimonials:**
Ask 3–5 recent buyers to share a quick reaction in the group about their order. Screenshot and repost on bdv-giant's seller profile.

**Game: Worst Card Ever Made (Comedy Round)**
> "Post the worst MTG card you know. Most votes = wins a prize."
Lightest engagement touchpoint of the two weeks — keeps energy high without fatigue.

**Deal: Refer & Earn**
> "Refer a friend to this group AND they make a purchase from bdv-giant → you both get $5 store credit."
Track via a simple "referred by [name]" note at checkout.

---

### Day 14 — Sunday: Close Strong
**Final Leaderboard + Prize Ceremony:**
- Post full 2-week leaderboard
- Award top 3 prizes (store credits: $20 / $10 / $5)
- Thank the group genuinely: names, wins, memorable moments from the games

**Teaser for Week 3:**
> "Week 3 starts Monday. Sealed product tournament. Drafting. New game format. Stay in."

This open loop keeps churn low after the campaign ends.

## Engagement Principles

1. **Post at consistent times.** 8PM games, 12PM weekday blitzes, 6–7PM Friday blitzes. Members will start anticipating and showing up early.
2. **Keep games short.** 15–60 minute windows. Longer windows kill urgency.
3. **Name winners publicly.** Social recognition drives re-engagement more than prizes.
4. **Never oversell.** Max 1 direct sales pitch per day. All other posts should be games, community, or education.
5. **Use countdowns.** "15 minutes left" messages consistently spike late activity.
6. **Make it exclusive.** "WhatsApp group only" framing is your strongest conversion lever with a warm audience.
7. **Track a leaderboard.** Even a simple one creates a competitive loop that dramatically increases retention.
